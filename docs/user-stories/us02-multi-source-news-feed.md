# US02: Multi-Source News Feed

## User Story

As a reader, I want to view news articles from different sources so that I can access multiple perspectives from one platform.

## Priority

10

## Estimate

4 days

## Iteration

Iteration 1

## Tasks Completed

- Created the `Article` model.
- Created the `Category` model.
- Created the `NewsSource` model.
- Registered models in Django admin.
- Added sample news data.
- Built the homepage article feed.
- Displayed article title, source, category, date, and summary.

## Evidence of Completion

The homepage displays news articles from different sources. Each article card shows key information such as the article title, news source, category, publication date, and summary.

## Testing Notes

The homepage was tested using sample article data to confirm that multiple articles and sources displayed correctly.
