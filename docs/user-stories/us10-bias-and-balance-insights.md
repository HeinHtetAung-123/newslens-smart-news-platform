# US10: Bias and Balance Insights

## User Story

As a reader, I want bias and balance insight for an article so that I can think more critically about wording and compare coverage before forming an opinion.

## Priority

40

## Estimate

5 days

## Iteration

Iteration 3

## Tasks Completed

- Added bias insight helper function.
- Checked article wording style.
- Counted strong/emotional words.
- Added source and category context.
- Added balance reminder.
- Displayed bias/balance panel on article detail page.

## Evidence of Completion

The article detail page includes a bias and balance insight panel. The panel shows source context, category context, wording style, and a reminder to compare sources.

## Testing Notes

Articles with neutral wording and stronger wording were tested to confirm that different insight messages appeared.
