# US04: Article Detail Page

## User Story

As a reader, I want to open an article detail page so that I can read more information about a story.

## Priority

10

## Estimate

3 days

## Iteration

Iteration 1

## Tasks Completed

- Created article detail URL route.
- Created article detail view.
- Created article detail template.
- Displayed article title, source, category, published date, description, quick summary, and preview content.
- Added original source link.
- Tested navigation from article cards to article detail pages.

## Evidence of Completion

Users can click an article card and open a detail page with more complete article information. The page also includes a button to open the original article source.

## Testing Notes

Article detail pages were tested from the homepage, category pages, and search results to confirm that the correct article opened each time.

## Final Status

Completed
